Terms of use

8 November 2026 · Mustafa Melih Turan · support@mantygames.net

Shelf Rush is a puzzle game. Use the game lawfully and do not interfere with other players, impersonate another person or exploit payments and rewards. Optional accounts must belong to you.

App Store purchases are handled by Apple under the terms shown at checkout. Coin packs are consumable virtual currency for use in Shelf Rush and have no cash withdrawal value. Restore purchases does not refill previously consumed coin packs. Deleting a game account deletes its saved inventory and does not initiate a refund. Your statutory consumer rights remain unaffected. Contact support for missing purchases; request refunds through Apple.

Guest saves are device-local. Do not remove the application before confirming a successful account backup if you want to retain progress. TestFlight builds may contain experimental features and their saves may require migration.
