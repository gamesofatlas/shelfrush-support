Privacy policy

Effective 13 November 2026. Shelf Rush is provided by Mustafa Melih Turan. Contact: mmelihturan@gmail.com.

What the game processes

  • On your device: routes and stars, interrupted games, coin balance, purchase fulfillment references, booster inventory, daily tasks, delivery achievements, market upgrades, language, sound and accessibility preferences.
  • Optional accounts and cloud saves, when enabled: a pseudonymous player ID and provider identifier; your game progress and inventory backup; route-action replays used to verify completion. When a social feature is available, its chosen display name, avatar and friend relationships are also processed. The game does not collect your sign-in password.
  • Purchases: Apple and Unity IAP process transaction/product information and technical data needed to operate purchases. Unity IAP 5.4 may collect purchase history, user/device or installation identifiers, product interactions, diagnostics and usage information. Country may be derived by the provider from the connection's IP address. We do not receive your full payment-card number.
  • Support: your email and the information you choose to send to resolve a problem or privacy request.

Optional Apple sign-in

Sign in with Apple is the only sign-in option in the current game. Apple authenticates you and Unity Authentication verifies the provider token to connect your game account. Guest play remains available. Google and Facebook sign-in are disabled.

Purpose and sharing

Data is used to provide and restore gameplay, authenticate optional accounts, fulfill purchases once, synchronize saves, prevent duplicate rewards, protect account integrity and answer support requests. It is processed where needed to provide the requested service, meet legal obligations, or protect legitimate security interests. We do not sell personal data. This release configuration does not enable advertising or personalized ad tracking, and the game does not request access to your contacts, microphone, camera or precise location.

Services include Apple for App Store purchases and optional Sign in with Apple, Unity for the game runtime, purchasing and optional Authentication/Cloud Code, and the game's server database when cloud saves are enabled. Relevant service privacy information: Apple, Unity. Service data may be processed outside your country under the providers' applicable contractual and legal safeguards. This support website is hosted by GitHub Pages; GitHub may process technical request logs. The site includes no advertising, tracking scripts or contact form.

Retention and deletion

Local saves remain until removed from the device or deleted through the account flow. Cloud account data is retained while the game account is active and removed through account deletion. A minimal deletion marker can remain while identity deletion is being retried, to prevent restoration of deleted data. Minimal transaction/replay-prevention references may remain where needed to prevent duplicate fulfillment or meet legal obligations. Provider transaction records and support correspondence follow their applicable retention obligations; support information is kept only as needed to resolve the request and related disputes. Contact us for the retention applicable to your specific request.

In supported builds, use My Profile → My Account → Delete game account. Account deletion removes the associated game save, including unused coin balance; it is not a refund. To request access, correction or deletion without the app, contact the email above. We may verify account ownership. A Unity Player Account is separate from the Shelf Rush game account: Unity account deletion; Unity privacy requests: unity-player-login-privacy@unity3d.com.

Your choices

You can play as a guest without creating a game account, keep optional online features unused, and change sound and supported motion settings. Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing, request portability, or complain to your local data protection authority. Email us to exercise these rights.

Children and changes

The optional account services are not intended for children below the age at which they may lawfully create an account without parental permission. If you believe a child has provided account information improperly, contact us. Parents can manage App Store purchases through Apple's device controls. We update this policy when the game's data practices change and show the revised date here. Account availability can differ between test and released builds; a disabled feature does not upload its associated game data.

Amaç, hizmetler ve paylaşım

Veriler oyunu sunma, kayıtları geri yükleme, hesap doğrulama, satın alımları bir kez teslim etme, eşitleme, mükerrer ödülü önleme, hesap güvenliği ve destek için işlenir. İşleme, talep ettiğin hizmetin sağlanmasına, yasal yükümlülüklere veya güvenliği koruyan meşru menfaatlere dayanır. Kişisel veri satmayız. Mevcut yapılandırmada reklam ve kişiselleştirilmiş reklam takibi kapalıdır. Oyun rehber, mikrofon, kamera veya kesin konum erişimi istemez.

Apple; App Store ve isteğe bağlı Apple ile giriş, Unity; oyun motoru, ödeme ve isteğe bağlı Authentication/Cloud Code, oyun veritabanı ise bulut kayıt hizmetini sağlar. Sağlayıcılar veriyi ülken dışında ilgili hukuki/sözleşmesel güvencelerle işleyebilir. Apple ve Unity gizlilik bilgilerini inceleyebilirsin. Bu destek sitesi GitHub Pages'te barındırılır; GitHub teknik bağlantı günlüklerini işleyebilir. Sitede reklam, izleme betiği veya iletişim formu yoktur.

Saklama ve silme

Yerel kayıtlar cihazdan kaldırılana veya hesap silme akışıyla temizlenene kadar kalır. Bulut kayıtları hesap etkin olduğu sürece tutulur ve hesap silmeyle kaldırılır. Kimlik silme yeniden denenirken, silinen verinin geri gelmesini engelleyen asgari bir silme işareti kalabilir. Mükerrer teslimi önlemek veya yasal yükümlülükleri karşılamak için gerekli asgari işlem referansları korunabilir. Sağlayıcıların işlem kayıtları kendi yükümlülüklerine tabidir. Destek yazışmaları talebi ve ilgili uyuşmazlığı çözmek için gerektiği sürece saklanır. Talebine uygulanacak saklama süresini iletişime geçerek öğrenebilirsin.

Desteklenen sürümlerde Profilim → Hesabım → Oyun hesabını sil yolunu kullan. Hesapla ilişkili ilerleme ve kullanılmamış coin bakiyesi silinir; bu işlem ücret iadesi değildir. Uygulamaya erişemiyorsan e-posta ile erişim, düzeltme veya silme isteyebilirsin. Şifre istemeden hesap sahipliğini doğrulamamız gerekebilir. Unity oyuncu hesabı ayrı bir kimliktir: Unity hesabını sil; kimlik gizliliği: unity-player-login-privacy@unity3d.com.
